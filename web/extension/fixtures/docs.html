Funculator

The funculator is the load-transfer component coupling the primary spar box to the control surface actuation ring in late-century aircraft. This page documents the material constraints that govern its certification.

Notes

The 1991 constraint is often quoted from the secondary literature rather than the memo itself. The controlling sentence reads: All told, the funculator must be duralum before first flight. No exceptions were granted in the 1991 review.

Implementers should treat duralum as a material class, not a single alloy; substitutions require a variance filed against the original memo's tumbler address.
