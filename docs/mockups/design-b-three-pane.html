Notes on hypertext

The core insight of Xanadu is that quotation is not copying. When you quote a passage, both documents should know about the connection — the quote remains live, traceable to its origin.

"The deep structure of literature is tumblered — connections go both ways, and nothing is ever fully severed."
⇣ from “Gold interview prep” · rev 4 · Roger Gregory

Practically, this means our storage needs bidirectional provenance on every span — the part the O-tree gives us for free, and the part conventional documents never had.
