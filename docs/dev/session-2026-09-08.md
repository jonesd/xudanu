## HT '26 proceedings digest (mined 2026-09-08)

52 papers, 4 demos, 5 workshops; London; proceedings live at
`sigweb.org/toc/ht26a.html` (abstracts free; PDFs via OpenTOC window
or ACM DL).

**Common directions:** (1) agentic-LLMs × hypertext is the topic —
three separate "new foundation" position papers; (2) provenance/
grounding anxiety — everyone wants evidence chains, nobody has them
structurally; (3) corpus analysis dominates, system-building is 1
session of 12; (4) spatial hypertext lineage alive (StoryMachine,
VASTE).

**Monolith verdict:** nobody builds Xanadu-shaped systems — all
fine-grained (small servers, bridges, layers-on-substrates). Our
positioning: *small server, complete model inside* — monolithic
model, modular surface.

**Must-cite neighbors:** Beaumont & Anderson (Seed Hypermedia —
transclusion/provenance/versioning theorized; closest neighbor);
Adamski et al. (H(G) hypertextality profile, rereads Xanadu
positively); Lupi et al. (Gardens→Landscapes); Rahdari et al.
(Hyperlayered); Sharma et al. (Edit-Distance Links, CRDTs); Millard
(friction in AI-augmented Zettelkasten — steering chair, cite and
engage the "right friction" tension); Revere & Blustein demo
(Transhierarchy); Bernstein (×2).

**Ideas worth stealing:** (1) self-report H(G) on our docuverse —
one figure nobody else can produce; (2) expose Xudanu as MCP/
hypermedia tool-server (Carson Gross's efficiency argument rides our
exact wave); (3) edit-distance links — diffs as navigable typed
links (an afternoon for us); (4) entropy monitoring of the backfollow
reuse graph; (5) phantom links (unanchored intentions) — FR-58
suggestions are phantom links becoming real.
